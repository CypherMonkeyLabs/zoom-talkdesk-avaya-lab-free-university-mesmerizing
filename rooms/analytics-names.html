T9 · T9-R26 · E911 and analytics

Analytics names, not fake charts

Analytics is where study notes usually go wrong, because a bar looks like a measurement. If the number is not on a page you opened, the bar is decoration and must be labeled that way. This lab's charts count rooms, tracks, and names that appeared in navigation.

Learn it in 5 minutes

  • Zoom's Contact Center navigation lists CX Insights and Analytics and reporting as labels on the product page.
  • Avaya's line says measure AI, agent, and customer outcomes.
  • Talkdesk's forecast API talks about queues and intervals, which is planning data, not a wallboard.
  • No page opened here published a service-level percentage.
  • A chart in this lab counts names and pages. It does not invent a KPI.

What is going on

Analytics is where study notes usually go wrong, because a bar looks like a measurement. If the number is not on a page you opened, the bar is decoration and must be labeled that way. This lab's charts count rooms, tracks, and names that appeared in navigation.

CX Insights is a Zoom label. It is not a definition of average handle time. Do not expand the abbreviation with a formula you did not read.

Outcomes on the Avaya line are a measurement intent. The unit is confirm.

Worked example

Build a bar for each name you saw: CX Insights, Quality Management, Workforce Management, AI Insights. The bar length is 1, meaning the name exists on a page. The axis says names, not percent.

Quiz

1. A bar chart in this lab may use a service level the author remembers.

False. Service level was not on an opened page. Charts here count names or repo objects.

2. Avaya's opened line says to measure outcomes.

True. The AI Insights line says measure AI, agent, and customer outcomes.
