# Contributing

Cypher Monkey, a lifelong student and AI builder, keeps this as study notes.

- Do not merge your own pull request. Leave it for review.
- Do not add LinkedIn, Spotify, income claims, trackers, or a CDN.
- Citation links belong in a sources list. Brand links are cyphermonkeylabs.com, github.com/CypherMonkeyLabs, and linktr.ee/cypherMonkey.
- Chat answers quote the index. Do not lower the confidence floor to raise a score.
- Run `node scripts/validate_lessons.mjs`, `python3 scripts/licence_manifest.py`, and `python3 scripts/csp_check.py` before you ask for review.
