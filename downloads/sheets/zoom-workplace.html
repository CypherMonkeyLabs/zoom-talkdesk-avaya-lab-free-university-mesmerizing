What Zoom Workplace names

  • Wikipedia's article, opened as Zoom Communications, calls the company a communications technology company in San Jose, known primarily for the videoconferencing platform Zoom.
  • The blog that opened is titled Say Hello to Zoom Workplace, an Evolution in the Zoom Platform Experience.
  • The same Zoom navigation lists Meetings, Chat, Phone, Mail and Calendar, Scheduler, Whiteboard, Clips, and Rooms under Zoom Workplace.
  • Zoom Contact Center sits under a Zoom CX heading on that navigation, beside Workforce Management, Quality Management, and Virtual Agent.
  • A marketing URL for Zoom Phone returned HTTP 404 in this session. The Phone name is still on the navigation and on the developer docs.
