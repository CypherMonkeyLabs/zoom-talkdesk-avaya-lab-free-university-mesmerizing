Workforce management, the general word

  • Wikipedia defines workforce management as a process to optimize productivity, competency, and engagement.
  • It says the process generally uses software rather than only a spreadsheet.
  • Activities named there include forecasting and scheduling.
  • Zoom's navigation lists Workforce Management under Zoom CX.
  • Talkdesk has a Workforce Management product named on its WFM API pages.
