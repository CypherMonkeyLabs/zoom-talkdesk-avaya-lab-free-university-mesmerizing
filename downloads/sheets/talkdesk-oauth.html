Talkdesk OAuth, account or user

  • The authentication page says Talkdesk supports OAuth 2.0.
  • Client Credentials are for account-level access, on behalf of an entire Talkdesk account.
  • Authorization Code is for user-level access, on behalf of a single user.
  • The page's front matter shows updatedAt 2026-09-10.
  • Client secrets do not belong in this lab.
