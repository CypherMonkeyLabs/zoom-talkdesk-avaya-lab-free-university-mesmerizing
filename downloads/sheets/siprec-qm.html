Recording architecture and quality scores

  • RFC 7245 describes architectures for recording media with SIP, including call centers.
  • It says some environments record for regulatory, compliance, and consumer protection reasons.
  • It does not name your statute.
  • RFC 7866 specifies SIP, SDP, and RTP to deliver media and metadata from a communication session to a recorder.
  • It defines a Session Recording Client on the path of the communication session and a Session Recording Server at the recording device, for active recording.
