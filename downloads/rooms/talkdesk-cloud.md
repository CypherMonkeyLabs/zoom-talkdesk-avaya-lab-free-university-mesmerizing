# Talkdesk as a cloud contact center

Study notes. Not vendor documentation and not legal advice.

- The Talkdesk home page title that opened calls it contact center software and AI customer experience automation.
- The cloud contact center URL opened with the title Cloud Contact Center Platform.
- The /platform/ path redirected to the contact center platform page.
- Developer docs describe public APIs and self-service products for customizing a contact center.
- A Wikipedia article titled Talkdesk returned HTTP 404.

Write three titles you can defend: the home title, the cloud contact center title, and Talkdesk Developers. Under them, write confirm for founding date, headquarters, and any customer logo you have not opened.
